Piano samples: University of Iowa Electronic Music Studios, Musical Instrument Samples (MIS),
Steinway & Sons model B, recorded by Lawrence Fritts. https://theremin.music.uiowa.edu/MISpiano.html

Terms stated by the source (https://theremin.music.uiowa.edu/MIS.html): the recordings are "freely
available on this website and may be downloaded and used for any projects, without restrictions."

Apophenia ships 30 of the mf notes (every minor third, B0 to C8; G7 in place of Gb7, whose file the
site doesn't serve), mixed to mono, aligned to the hammer, level-matched, trimmed to 2.5-7 s with a
0.3 s fade, as 16-bit 44.1 kHz WAV. The tool that made them is tools/piano/PianoPrep.java.
