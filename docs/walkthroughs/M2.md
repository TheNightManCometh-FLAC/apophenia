# M2 (builds 0.2.0–0.2.5): projects, Board, stacks — phone walkthrough

Device: Galaxy S22 Ultra (SM-S908U), Android 16. Install `Apophenia-0.2.0-debug.zip` over 0.1.2 (unzip first). Everything you recorded is kept; the database moves to schema v3 on first launch.

## 1. No project yet
1. Open the app. The top left says **NO PROJECT ▾**. The Board says NO PROJECT OPEN and offers **+ NEW PROJECT**, **● RECORD** (to the Library) and **LIBRARY ›**.
2. Tap **+ NEW PROJECT**. MAKE IT stays grey until you type a name. Type "Low Tide" and tap MAKE IT. The top bar says LOW TIDE and the Board is empty, with **● RECORD HERE** and **ADD FROM LIBRARY ›**.

## 2. Recording into the project
1. Tap **● RECORD HERE**. The recording screen says **TO LOW TIDE**. Play something, then tap ■ STOP.
2. On the save sheet, **GOES TO** says "Low Tide + Library". Tap it: it changes to "Low Tide only". Tap it again to switch back, or leave it.
3. Close the sheet. The take is a window on the Board. In LIBRARY it's there (unless you chose "only"), and its row says "in Low Tide".
4. The top **● REC** still records to the Library, whichever screen you're on.

## 3. Windows
1. Record or add three or four things. New windows fill two columns, left to right.
2. **Tap** a window: the recording opens full size. ‹ brings you back to the Board.
3. **Hold** a window until the phone buzzes, then drag it: it moves, with a copper outline. A plain drag on the Board scrolls it.
4. Drag the **corner** of a window (the two small lines, bottom right) to resize it.
5. Leave the app and come back, or switch to another project and back: every window is where you left it.
6. Press ▶ on that recording in the player or the Library: its window's waveform shows the playback position.

## 4. Adding from the Library
1. Board ⋯ → **Add from the Library**. The Library opens with "ADD TO LOW TIDE" at the top. Tap rows to pick them, then **ADD n**. You're back on the Board with them there.
2. In the Library, long-press a row, pick a few more, **ACTIONS ▾ → Add to project**. Tap a project to add them; "✓ ON ITS BOARD" shows. Tap it again to take them off (UNDO puts them back where they were). **NEW** makes a project with them in it.
3. A recording's full view has a **PROJECTS** row doing the same for that one.
4. Put a scrap that's in a Group onto a project. The bar says it's also in that Group and offers **REMOVE FROM GROUPS**. Tap it: it leaves its Groups (UNDO puts it back).

## 5. Switching, renaming, deleting
1. Make a second project. Tap the project name at the top: the menu lists both (✓ OPEN on the current one, how many are on each Board), plus New project, Rename, Delete.
2. Pick the other project: its Board opens. Then pick it from the Library or Tools: you're taken to its Board.
3. **Rename** it.
4. **Delete** it: the bar says its scraps stay in the Library, with UNDO. Without UNDO, it's in LIBRARY ⋯ → Trash as a project, with RESTORE; its Board comes back as it was.
5. Close the app with a project open and open it again: it opens on that Board.

## Not in this build
Stacks, + STACK and recording over a stack (M2 part 3). The window menu (snap, stand mode, show by type) from C4 is not there yet. Lyrics, text and chord charts arrived in 0.2.1 (section 6).

## 6. Added in 0.2.1: lyrics, text notes, chord charts
1. On a project's Board: ⋯ → **Write lyrics**. The editor opens. Type `[Dm7]Salt on the [G]window, the` (the **[ CHORD ]** key puts the brackets in), a **SECTION** line, a few more lines. Tap **DONE**: the chords sit over the words.
2. **▶ SCROLL** starts the auto-scroll; **SPEED ▾** picks 1–6; ⋯ changes text size or hides the chords.
3. **UNDO / REDO** in the editor step through every change. Nothing needs saving; leave and come back and it's there.
4. ⋯ → **Write chord chart**: `| F / C / | G / Dm7 / |` (keys for | / % SECTION METER). DONE shows the bars; a bar with the wrong number of beats is outlined in copper with a note.
5. ⋯ → **Write text note**: it opens straight into typing.
6. Back on the Board each one is a window showing its first words or bars. In LIBRARY they're rows titled by their first words, and FIND finds words inside them.
7. Add that lyric to a second project and open it there: the header says it's that project's **own copy**. Change it there; the first project's version doesn't change.

## 7. Added in 0.2.2: stacks (headphones on)
1. On a Board, tap **+ STACK** on two or three recording windows. The first time, the player bar becomes STACK 1 · LOW TIDE. The windows now say IN STACK.
2. Tap the player bar: the stack opens. **▶ PLAY**: the layers loop together. Listen at the loop points: no click.
3. Per layer: **LOOP ▾** (loop, one-shot, free time), **− / +** for level, **M** mute, **S** solo. Change them while it plays: no clicks.
4. ⋯ → **Add a layer**: the Library shows recordings only; pick some, **ADD n**. Your Library filter comes back afterwards.
5. With the stack playing, press the top **● REC** and play along. The recording screen says NEW LAYER; on the speaker it warns that the stack will leak in. Stop: the take is a new one-shot layer starting where you pressed ● REC. (It isn't latency-corrected yet, so expect it slightly late.)
6. **● BOUNCE**: the stack's own sound, no mic, saved as "Stack 1 mix" with the save sheet.
7. Open another project's Board and tap **+ STACK** there: it asks before replacing the stack in the player.

## 8. Added in 0.2.3: conform and importing
1. Give two recordings different BPMs and keys (their BPM and KEY rows), put both in a stack. The first one sets the stack's tempo and key (header button, e.g. "92 · D DOR ▾"). Under the second layer: RENDERING… then CONFORMED +2 st · 96→92 BPM; it now plays in time and in key. Tap that line to turn conform off and back on.
2. Header button → set the stack's own tempo or key: every loop and one-shot layer follows. Free-time layers never move.
3. LIBRARY ⋯ → **Import from the phone**: pick an MP3 or M4A from Downloads, and a .txt. The song becomes a recording (tagged "imported"), the text a note. Board ⋯ → Import puts them in that project.

## 9. Added in 0.2.4: overdub latency
1. TOOLS → **OVERDUB LATENCY**. On the speaker, volume up, quiet room: **MEASURE ON PHONE SPEAKER**. Five bursts; it shows the round trip (ms) and saves it.
2. With wired headphones (and then Bluetooth): hold an earbud against the bottom of the phone, measure again. Each route keeps its own value.
3. Play a stack with a clear beat and record yourself clapping along with the top ● REC: the new layer should now line up with the beat when played back.

## 10. Added in 0.2.5: lyric sync (M2 complete)
1. Record yourself singing a lyric you've written (or import a song). Play it, then open the lyric: ⋯ → **Sync lines to the recording playing**. It restarts; tap the big bar as each line starts. STOP if you only want part of it.
2. Play that recording again with the lyric open: the line being sung lights up and stays a third of the way down. ⋯ → Sync earlier/later moves it by 0.1 s.
3. M2's test from the plan: a stack of three of your recordings at different tempos, sing over it while reading a lyric on the Board, keep takes B and D.
