# M3 walkthrough: Tools (S22 Ultra, Android 16)

Builds: 0.3.0–0.3.6 (M3 is code-complete). Try with headphones and on the speaker. Nothing here has run on the phone yet.

## 1. Metronome (0.3.0)
1. TOOLS (bottom) › Metronome. Drag sideways on the big number: it moves 1 BPM per finger-width step. Tap it, type 133, Done.
2. ▶ START. Tap TAP in time a few times: the tempo follows the median of your taps.
3. Tap beat lights: each goes strong › accent › normal › silent (outlined). The silent beat is skipped, the bar keeps its length.
4. Meter ▾ › 7/8 · 2+2+3: three beat lights, the last one wider; BPM now counts eighths. Custom… › `3+3+2/8`.
5. Subdivision › Triplets, then Sixteenths with Swing › Triplet (67%): the off sixteenths lean back.
6. Polyrhythm › 3 against 4: a higher, different click spread across the bar.
7. Count-in › 1 bar, Start: "COUNT-IN · 1 TO GO", then bar 1.
8. Tempo trainer › +5 BPM every 4 bars, stops at +30: the number climbs every 4 bars and stops.
9. Silent bars › 2 on, 1 off: every third bar is silent, the bar count and beat lights keep going.
10. The top bar's CLICK ▾ − / + change the same metronome; CLICK ▾ › Full metronome opens this screen.
11. With the click running, ● REC, play along for 20 s, stop. The take keeps the click grid (not shown yet; it's in the database for analysis and exports).
12. Kill the app and reopen: every setting is still there.

## 2. Drum rudiments (0.3.1: Learn › Reference)
1. LEARN (bottom) › 16. Single Paradiddle. ▶ HEAR IT: four times through on a snare, R L R R L R L L lit as it plays, accents louder.
2. Tempo › 120 and hear it again. Try 20. Flam, 31. Drag, 4. Multiple Bounce Roll.
3. (On hold: the stickings, especially 8, 23, 27 and 30, wait for a trained drummer to check them.) Rhythm is simplified to equal steps.

## 2b. Drum grooves (0.3.2: Learn › Reference)
1. LEARN › Rock beat › ▶ HEAR IT: 8 bars, the step being played lit across HH / SD / BD.
2. Jazz swing: ride "ding, ding-a ding", hat foot on 2 and 4. Boom bap: the swung hats. Trap: the hat roll at the end of the bar.
3. Tempo › change it and hear it again. Say which grooves look or sound wrong, and which styles you want added.

## 3. Tuner (0.3.0)
1. TOOLS ▾ (top bar) › Tuner. The mic chip says TUNER · MIC ON. Play your bass's open strings: note name, cents, a 21-light meter (middle light green within ±2.5 ¢), Hz, target and MIDI number.
2. Listen for ▾ › Bass drop D, detune the E string to D: it reads D1.
3. Drop to the lowest note you've got (drop A or a low B): does it hold steady? (**[measure]**)
4. A4 › 442: everything reads about 8 ¢ flat at 440.
5. Temperament › Just, Tonic D: an F♯ tuned to sound sweet over D reads near 0 ¢ (about 14 ¢ flat of equal).
6. Spell notes in › E♭ major: the black keys read as flats; E major: as sharps.
7. Leave the tuner: the chip goes back to MIC OFF.

## 4. Drone (0.3.0)
1. TOOLS ▾ (top bar) › Drone starts it straight away; the TOOLS label gets a dot. Same again stops it (fades out).
2. Tools › Drone: root, octave, fifth, third, octave above. Changes apply while it plays.
3. In the tuner, Drone › ▶ PLAY: tune a note against it.

## 5. Top-bar slots (0.3.0)
1. Tools › Top bar slot 1 › Metronome. TOOLS ▾ now lists Metronome and Drone.
## 6. Playback speed (0.3.3)
1. Import a song (Library ⋯ › Import) and ▶ it. TOOLS › Playback speed.
2. Speed › 0.60×: same pitch, slower. ±0.05 nudges it while it plays, with no click or gap.
3. Pitch › −2 st, fine tune › +15 ¢. The player bar says 0.60× · −2 st · +15¢.
4. Play to the start of a bass line: SET A. Let it run to the end of the phrase: SET B. It loops, with no click at the seam.
5. Step-up › +0.05 each pass, up to 1.00×: the speed rises each pass (PASS n · NOW 0.70× under the waveform).
6. Set the song's BPM on its recording, then count-in each pass › 4 beats: four clicks at the slowed tempo before each pass.
7. + MARK HERE, SAVE LOOP. Leave and come back: the marks and the saved loop are listed, tap one to go there or loop it.
8. With the loop running at 0.6×, turn the click on and ● REC: play your version (plan §13 M3 goal, isolator still to come).

## 7. Recorder markers and clipping (0.3.3)
1. ● REC, tap ◆ MARK a few times while playing.
2. Play loud right into the mic: CLIPPING · MOVE BACK OR PLAY SOFTER shows.
3. Stop, ▶ the take, open Playback speed: the markers are there as Marker 1, 2…

## 8. Isolator and note finder (0.3.4: the M3 goal)
1. Import a stereo song (it now stays stereo) and ▶ it. TOOLS › Isolator.
2. Preset › Bass. Tap BYPASSED ⇄ ISOLATING while it plays: the same place, with and without, no click.
3. Dial it in: tone ⇄ hit toward tone, high cut 350 Hz, stereo position keep, centre, how wide.
4. Remove the centre on a song with a centred vocal: the vocal drops (a karaoke effect).
5. Note finder: pause on a bass note. The strongest notes show by name, lit on the piano and as dots on the tuner's tuning (set the tuner to Bass E standard or drop D for your bass).
6. The milestone goal: Playback speed 0.60×, loop the bass line, Isolator on, then click on and ● REC your version.


## 9. Practice timer (0.3.5)
1. TOOLS › Practice timer › ON › the recording in the player (or the project, or type "Paradiddles at 100"). START, PAUSE, GO ON.
2. Close the app for a minute and come back: the timer kept running.
3. Add a note, STOP & LOG: it is in the history with the note; TODAY and 7 DAYS add it up. ✕ removes one (UNDO puts it back).

## 10. Drum chart (0.3.6)
1. Set the click to 3/4 at 90, then on a project's Board use WRITE ▾ › Drum chart. It opens on an empty bar of 3/4 (HH, SD, BD), at 90 BPM.
2. Tap cells: each tap goes x → X → g → (o on HH) → empty. UNDO and REDO step back and forward.
3. Write a beat, then ▶ HEAR IT: it loops on the kit with the playing step lit. ■ STOP.
4. Use the menus. METER 4/4. STEPS eighths, then back to sixteenths: the eighths stay. BARS 2. SWING 58%. ADD A DRUM › Floor tom. Tap SD › Remove the snare.
5. TEXT: the chart as drum tab. Type a mistake (`ZZ |x---|`): it's listed in copper. GRID again shows the rest of the chart.
6. ‹ back: the Board window shows the tab; the Library row is tagged DRM. TEMPO › opens the BPM sheet.
