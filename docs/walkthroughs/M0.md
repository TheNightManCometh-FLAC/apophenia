# M0 walkthrough — Device Lab (build 0.0.2, debug)

The mockups part of M0 gets its own section when they exist. This covers the Device Lab (official plan §14.2). Phone: Galaxy S22 Ultra (SM-S908U), Android 16.

## Install
1. Unzip `apophenia-0.0.2-debug.zip` and open the APK. It installs over 0.0.1.
2. There are now two icons: **Apophenia** (the scaffold screen) and **Apophenia Lab**. Debug builds only; a release build has no Lab icon and no microphone permission.
3. Open **Apophenia Lab** and tap **Allow microphone**. Check: no mic indicator (green dot) while no test is running.

## 1 · Round-trip latency — once per route
Media volume around 60–70 %. Each run plays five short noise bursts; the mic dot shows only while it runs.
1. **Speaker:** nothing connected, phone flat on a table, quiet room. Tap **Measure latency**.
2. **Wired:** USB-C headphones/dongle. Hold one earpiece against the bottom mic hole. Run.
3. **Bluetooth:** your usual earbuds/headphones. Hold one earpiece against the bottom mic. Run.

Each result shows the route, five burst times, the median and the spread. Good run: all five found, confidence (c=) well above 8, spread within ±1 ms. "NOT FOUND" means too quiet or too noisy: raise the volume and repeat. Repeat any run that says "route changed".

## 2 · Microphone sources
1. Silent room, phone still: **Silent room**. It records 6 s from UNPROCESSED, then VOICE_RECOGNITION, then MIC.
2. Bass in D standard, phone ~30 cm from the amp or the bass body: tap **Instrument** and let the open D (or open E/drop-A low string) ring through all three (~20 s total; re-pluck as it decays).
3. Listen to the six WAVs in **Music/Apophenia Device Lab** (Samsung My Files → Music) on headphones. Note which sounds cleanest and fullest in the low end, and whether any source pumps or gates.

## 3 · Play + record + click, battery and heat
1. Unplug the charger. Pick **10 min**, tap **Start**. You hear a quiet pad and a 120 BPM click; the mic records to a temp file. Leave the app in front with the screen on.
2. Do it once on the speaker and once on headphones.
3. Target: output and input xruns 0, short reads 0, writer overflow 0, frames out ≈ 100 % of wall clock. Listen for any dropout in the click.
4. Optional: the **30 min** run, for battery and heat.
5. Interruption: start a 1 min run, then press Home. The run stops and logs "Stopped early". (Screen-off running is an M1 foreground-service feature.)

## 4 · Glyphs
Look at the line `𝄪 𝄫 ♯ ♭ ♮ C𝄪 B𝄫 F♯m7♭5`: each symbol should be a real music glyph, not a box or a letter. Tap **Log glyph check**.

## Send the results
Tap **Share log** and send it back (paste into the chat, or save it to a file you attach). Also say which mic source sounded best to you.

## Forced kill
During a 1 min stability run, swipe the Lab away in Recents. Reopen it: earlier results are still in the log; the mic dot is gone.
