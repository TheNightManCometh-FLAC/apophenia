# M7 walkthrough — Exports and release

Phone: Samsung Galaxy S22 Ultra (SM-S908U), Android 16.

## 1. MusicXML export (build 0.7.0)
1. Open a tab scrap (or make one: a few notes on the guitar, a chord symbol, a tied note, a triplet).
2. ⋯ › MusicXML. The save picker opens with the scrap's title and `.musicxml`; save it to Downloads. A notice says "MusicXML file saved."
3. On the PC: open it in MuseScore 4 (free). Check: notation on a treble-8vb clef and tab under it with the same frets; the tied note, the triplet bracket, the chord symbol above.
4. A sheet-music scrap with lyrics and a 1st/2nd ending: the same in MuseScore, lyrics hyphenated, endings in place.
5. Cancel the save picker once: nothing is written, no notice.

## 2. MIDI, text tab, ChordPro (build 0.7.1)
1. The same tab scrap › ⋯ › MIDI: save, open in FL Studio (drag into the playlist or File › Import › MIDI): one track a part, the tempo right, a 7/8 or 6/8 meter shown right; tied notes are single notes; a repeat plays out.
2. ⋯ › Text tab: the .txt opens in any editor; six string lines a system, frets in place.
3. A lyric scrap › ⋯ › Export ChordPro: `{title}` and `{key}` at the top, chords in brackets.
4. A chord chart › ⋯ › Export ChordPro: one grid per section, `.` on held beats.

## 3. Licences and privacy (build 0.7.2)
1. LIBRARY ⋯ › Licences and privacy: the PRIVACY paragraph, then 14 rows.
2. Tap Signalsmith Stretch: its MIT text opens under it; tap again to close.
3. Tap LiteRT: its licence, then the third-party notices' first part with a note on where the rest is. Scrolling stays smooth.
4. Back (‹ or the system back) returns to the Library.

## 4. BAM splash (build 0.7.3)
1. Swipe Apophenia out of recents, then open it from the launcher: the launcher icon on black (Android's own splash), then the Better App Mafia mark fades in, holds about a second and a half, and fades out to the Board. No white flash at any point.
2. Open it cold again and tap the mark: it fades out at once.
3. Rotate the phone, or leave the app and come back: no splash.
4. Use the Record widget with the app closed: no splash; the recording screen shows straight away.

## 5. MusicXML import (build 0.7.4)
1. Export a tab scrap as MusicXML (§1), then LIBRARY › import that file: a tab scrap with the same title opens, notes and frets as before.
2. From MuseScore 4 on the PC: File › Export › MusicXML (both .mxl and uncompressed). Copy both to the phone and import them. A piano piece comes in as two parts (upper, lower); a guitar piece with tab comes in as one tab part with the right tuning.
3. Import a .xml that isn't music (any other XML file): "Not imported: It isn't a MusicXML score."
4. The import notice lists anything a file was read without (slurs, hairpins).

## 6. MIDI import (build 0.7.5)
1. Export a tab or sheet scrap as MIDI (§2), then LIBRARY › import it: the same notes come back (repeats written out); a guitar or bass part comes back as tab.
2. From FL Studio: export a pattern with drums, bass (a bass plugin on program 34) and keys as MIDI (File › Export › MIDI file), copy it to the phone and import it. One part per channel; the drums in their own part; the playing snapped to sixteenths; a triplet fill written as triplets.
3. Open the imported scrap in the piano roll: notes where FL Studio had them, to the sixteenth.

## 7. FL Studio package (build 0.7.6)
1. A project with a stack of two or three layers (one loop, one one-shot, one conformed to the stack's tempo), a tab scrap and a chord chart on its Board.
2. Board ⋯ › Export for FL Studio › Stems and original takes. Save the .zip to Downloads; the notice says how many stems, takes and MIDI files went in.
3. On the PC, unzip it. README.txt first: tempo, key, each layer's start.
4. FL Studio: set the tempo from the README, drag everything in Stems/ onto the Playlist at bar 1: the layers line up as they did on the phone, loops repeated, the conformed one at the stack's tempo. Mix.wav plays the same.
5. File › Import › MIDI file for each file in MIDI/: the tab's notes, tempo and meter; the chord chart as chords. Note whether FL Studio kept the markers and any meter change (plan §11 [measure]).
6. Stack ⋯ › Export for FL Studio › Original takes only: no Stems/ folder, takes untouched.
7. Mute a layer and export again: its stem is still there (stems ignore mute); Mix.wav leaves it out.

## 8. No network, and transcription still works (build 0.7.7)
1. Install 0.7.7 over 0.7.6. Android Settings › Apps › Apophenia › Permissions: only Microphone (and nothing under "Other permissions" about the network).
2. A recording › AI ▾ › Notes (Basic Pitch): it transcribes as before. This build drops Google Play libraries LiteRT had pulled in; if transcription fails, that's the cause.

## 9. The icon (build 0.7.8)
1. Install the 0.7.8 debug zip over 0.7.7. The launcher shows the new icon: the glowing staff on black, nothing cut off at the edges of One UI's rounded square.
2. Settings › Display › (icon shape, if your launcher has one) to another shape: still whole.
3. Recents and the app's long-press shortcut (Record) show it too.

## 10. Ear: practice and high scores (build 0.7.9)
1. LEARN › Ear: PRACTICE and HIGH SCORES at the top. Under PRACTICE, "Mixed: everything" starts at once; Intervals (and the other types) open on their drills.
2. Practise any drill: STREAK counts up with each right answer and drops to 0 on a wrong one; BEST shows the drill's high score and turns into NEW BEST when you pass it.
3. Back out and HIGH SCORES lists the drill with your best run. Tap it: straight back into practice.

## 11. Navigation B and + NEW (build 0.8.0)
1. The bottom bar has three tabs: MAKE, PRACTICE, STUDY. Each shows its sub-tabs along the top (MAKE: BOARD · LIBRARY; PRACTICE: TOOLS · EAR; STUDY: COURSE · REFERENCE · AI).
2. Go to PRACTICE › EAR, then MAKE, then PRACTICE again: it reopens on EAR.
3. In a Course lesson, tap a link to an Ear drill: it opens under PRACTICE › EAR. Back out, tap STUDY: the lesson is still there.
4. The strip at the top: CLICK ▾ · TOOLS ▾ · ● REC · + NEW, on every screen.
5. With a project open, on its Board, tap + NEW: the top says INTO <PROJECT> + LIBRARY. Pick Lyrics: the lyric editor opens, and the scrap is on the Board and in the Library.
6. From the Library (or any other screen), + NEW starts on INTO LIBRARY. Pick Guitar tab: the tab editor opens; the tab is in the Library only. Switch to the project option and pick Record: the recorder says TO <PROJECT>.
7. Try each type once: Record, Lyrics, Text note, Chord chart, Drum chart, Guitar tab, Bass tab, Sheet music, Import.
8. (0.8.1) + NEW › Piano roll: new sheet music opens in the piano roll. Draw a few notes, then VIEW ▾ › Notation: the same notes on the staff. It's in the Library (and the project, if you picked that).
9. (0.8.2) Open a project's Board: the fragments are squares, staggered and slightly tilted, not rows. Each starts with its kind (RECORDING, LYRICS, TAB…) as a filled tag, then your tags. Tap one: its full view opens. Hold and drag one somewhere else, leave the Board and come back: it stays there.
10. (0.8.2) MAKE has BOARD · LIBRARY · AI; STUDY has COURSE · REFERENCE.
11. (0.8.3) Tap ⚙ at the top right: Settings. Pick each theme in turn and look at the Board, the Library and a tab. Paper is light: the status bar icons should turn dark. Close and reopen the app: the theme stays.
12. (0.8.3) Change CHORD SYMBOLS › Show as to Jazz in Settings, then open a chord chart: the symbols are jazz. Change A4 to 442: the tuner shows 442.
13. (0.8.4) PRACTICE › EAR › Intervals: under the drills, PICK YOUR OWN. Tap m3 and P5, then PRACTISE THESE 2: only those two come up. Back out: HIGH SCORES lists "Your pick: m3 · P5". Try it in Chords and Scales too.
14. (0.8.4) The home screen icon is the new painting. Check it in the app drawer and on the home screen.
15. (0.8.5) Sheet music › ADD ▾ › clef: Tenor; key A major: three sharps, F♯ on the second line, C♯ on the fourth, G♯ in the second space. Key E♭ major: three flats, B♭ in the third space, E♭ on the top line, A♭ on the middle line.
16. (0.8.6) In the piano roll, SNAP ▾ › 1/8 triplet; draw three notes across beat 1. VIEW ▾ › Notation: a bracketed 3 over three eighths.
17. (0.8.6) TOOL ▾ › Chord stamp, CHORD ▾ › Seventh › maj7; tap a row: four notes at once, sounding. Pick a 7♯9 and stamp it on E: notation shows F𝄪 on top.
18. (0.8.7) Stamp a chord a half note long, SNAP 1/16, TOOLS ▾ › Arpeggiate: up and down. It becomes eight sixteenths going up and back; ▶ plays them; notation shows the sixteenths.
19. (0.8.8) Piano roll, TOOL ▾ › Paint, SNAP 1/16: drag along a row, then drift up a few rows: a run of sixteenths follows your finger. TOOL ▾ › Slice: tap the middle of a long note: it splits. In a score with two parts (e.g. an imported MIDI), the other part shows as outlines; GHOSTS hides them.
20. (0.8.9) MAKE › AI: "Sheet music and tab" and "Performance check" both say READY. Tap Performance check. REFERENCE ▾: pick a melody you wrote (sheet music or tab, or an imported MIDI's part). Record yourself singing or playing it (● REC), then YOUR TAKE ▾: that take. CHECK. Sing one note deliberately flat and one wrong, and come in late on another: each should be listed with its bar, ▶ YOURS loops your stretch, ▶ REFERENCE plays the written notes. Try it slower than written: tempo alone shouldn't count against you.
