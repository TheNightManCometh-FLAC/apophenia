# M6 walkthrough — Theory and Pitch Trainer (Learn)

Phone: Samsung Galaxy S22 Ultra (SM-S908U), Android 16. Headphones for the piano sound, then the speaker once.

## Start here: a 20-minute pass through M6 (build 0.6.21)
M6 is code-complete. The numbered sections below are the per-build detail; this pass covers what matters most, in the order that finds problems fastest. Note anything wrong with its section number.

1. **Notation drawn at all (never seen yet, 0.5.2 onward).** LEARN › COURSE › Reading › The staff and clefs: middle C in treble, bass and alto clef. Then Reading tab: staff with tab under it. If staves, clefs or accidentals look wrong or blank, stop here and say so: most of the Course's pictures use the same drawing (§16).
2. **A Reference chord page** (§1): FIND `E7#9`, SHOW ON guitar / piano / staff, ▶ the chord, ROOT ▾ to G♭.
3. **Guitar tunings** (§18–19): Reference › Guitar tunings › Modal and suspended › DADGAD; USE FOR THE REFERENCE NECK; back on the E7♯9 page the neck is in DADGAD. Set it back to Standard.
4. **Named progressions** (§20): Jazz › Coltrane changes; tap chords, ▶ PLAY IT, ROOT ▾ E♭.
5. **Ear** (§3–8, §22): EAR › MIXED › P, five questions; ANSWER ▾ on your guitar for an interval; then CHORDS › Voicing spread. Then a sung drill (Pitch › sing or play the note): the mic opens only for it.
6. **The Course** (§9–17): one page from each of three categories (Sound & pitch › harmonic series, Harmony › ii–V–I, Instruments › CAGED), each with ▶ HEAR, TRY's KEY ▾ and CHECK BY EAR.
7. **IN YOURS** (§2, §20): with a chord chart holding Em7 A7 Dmaj7, the ii–V–I progression page and the m7 chord page list it.
8. Once with the speaker instead of headphones: the piano sound level against the click and the drone.

What M6 can't show yet, by design: Exercises (M7, your content), exports (M7).

## 2. In your material, naming a chord (build 0.6.1)

4. Open Intervals → Minor third: IN YOURS lists the m3 steps in your tab or sheet music with part and bar.

## 3. Ear: intervals and chords (build 0.6.2)
1. Learn → LEARN ▾ → Ear. Chips MIXED · INTERVALS · CHORDS; MIXED shows three mixed drills.
2. INTERVALS → Sixths and sevenths → **P**. It plays at once. ▶ PLAY repeats it. Answer wrong on purpose: red on yours, copper on the right one, ANSWER explains it ("m6, minor sixth: 8 semitones, C up to A♭ (played up)"). ▶ RIGHT ONE and ▶ YOURS play both on the same root. NEXT ›. SESSION counts and the streak.
3. SOUND ▾ → Down, then Together: the next questions are descending, then harmonic. Random key off: everything starts on C.
4. ANSWER ▾ → On the piano: the given note is lit; tap the other one, ▶ MINE, CHECK. → On your guitar: tap a fret on one string.
5. CHORDS → Maj7 · m7 · 7 → **T**: twenty questions, then the score. Back on the Ear list the row shows BEST TEST n/20 · 1 SESSION. AGAIN starts a new test.
6. CHORDS → Triad inversions → **L**: tap each answer to hear root position, 1st and 2nd inversion on one root; ▶ NEW ROOT.
7. CHORDS → Every seventh chord → P, ANSWER ▾ → On the piano: the bass is given; tap the other three notes in any octave; CHECK.

## 4. Pitch, scale degrees, scales, singing (build 0.6.3)
1. Ear → PITCH → Name the note, after an A → P: you hear A4, then a note; answer by name. ANSWER ▾ → On the piano: tap that note in any octave, CHECK.
2. PITCH → Higher or lower · 10 cents → P: two plain tones; Higher or Lower. Try the 5-cent one.
3. PITCH → Which octave → P: answers C2–B2 … C6–B6.
4. PITCH → Sing or play the note → P. Wait for the note to stop, tap ● LISTEN (the mic opens now, not before), sing or play it on bass or guitar. It shows the note it hears and the cents; after a moment it grades it and the mic closes. Sing it a fifth off on purpose: it says how many cents off.
5. INTERVALS → Sing the interval → P: "Sing a M3 above the note you heard"; ● LISTEN.
6. SCALE DEGREES → Degrees in a major key → P: a cadence, then a note; answer the degree. SOUND ▾ → Over a drone: a drone of the tonic and the note instead.
7. SCALES → Modes of major → P: a drone and the scale up and down; answer the mode. Characteristic notes: tonic, the mode's note, tonic, then 3rd, 5th, the note, tonic.
8. MIXED → Mixed: everything: questions from all by-name drills; the buttons are each question's own.

## 5. Harmony and tuning (build 0.6.4)
Headphones for the tuning drills.
1. Ear → HARMONY chip → Chords in a major key → P: a cadence, then one chord; answer its numeral. Minor key the same.
2. Cadences → P: four chords; Authentic, Plagal, Half, Deceptive. Check an answer's explanation lists the four chords with their notes.
3. Secondary dominants → P: e.g. "V7/V (D F♯ A C) → V (G B D)" in C.
4. Neapolitan and augmented sixths → P: listen to the outer voices of the +6 chords opening out to the octave on V.
5. Tritone and backdoor substitutes → P: ii7, then V7 / ♭II7 / ♭VII7, then Imaj7.
6. Where it modulates → P: the home cadence, then the new key's V7 – I – IV – V7 – I; "To vi (relative minor)" and so on.
7. TUNING AND TIMBRE → Equal or just; Beating; Which harmonic.
8. Check labels: no stray ♭ in words anywhere (e.g. Reference → a scale page → ▶ VS ▾ shows "Bebop dominant", not "♭e♭op").
