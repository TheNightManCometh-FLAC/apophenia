# Learn — text and example sources, licences checked (21 Oct 2026)

Apophenia is GPL-3.0 and ships on Google Play, GitHub and F-Droid. A source is usable **inside the app** only if its licence allows redistribution in a GPL app. Anything non-commercial, or "all rights reserved", is out. Licences are as read on 21 Oct 2026 from the publishers' and Open Textbook Library pages; re-check each one before adapting from it.

| Source | Licence | Use in Apophenia |
|---|---|---|
| **Open Music Theory** (Gotham, Gullings, Hamm, Hughes, Jarvis, Lavengood, Peterson; VIVA Pressbooks) | CC BY-SA 4.0 | **Yes**: adapt with attribution and a modification notice. CC lists BY-SA 4.0 as one-way compatible with GPL-3.0. Broadest open coverage: fundamentals, diatonic and chromatic harmony, form, pop/rock and jazz chapters, post-tonal. |
| **Understanding Basic Music Theory** (Schmidt-Jones, OpenStax CNX) | CC BY (2.0 in the original PDF; the current version is unconfirmed) | **Yes**, with attribution. Plain first-step explanations of pitch, rhythm, acoustics and the harmonic series. |
| **Music Theory for the 21st-Century Classroom** (Hutchinson) | GNU Free Documentation License 1.2+ | **Fact-checking only.** Mixing GFDL text into GPL content is not clean. |
| **Fundamentals, Function, and Form** (Mount, Milne Open Textbooks) | CC BY-NC 4.0 (the publisher's page) | **No**: non-commercial is incompatible with GPL and Play distribution. |
| **Rick Beato's courses** | Proprietary | **Design only** (organisation and lesson modes), never content. |
| Standard texts: Kostka/Payne/Almén *Tonal Harmony*; Aldwell/Schachter/Cadwallader *Harmony and Voice Leading*; Levine *The Jazz Theory Book*; Persichetti *Twentieth-Century Harmony* | Copyrighted | **Fact-checking and disputes only.** Facts and terminology are free to use; their wording and examples are not. |
| McGill Billboard chord annotations; CC BY parts of ChoCo (already in plan §14.1) | CC0 / CC BY 4.0 | **Yes**: real-song progression data for "where it occurs" (song names and chords, no audio). |
| Apophenia's own music core (catalog of 50 scales and 41 chords, spelling, MusicXML) | GPL-3.0 (ours) | **The backbone of Reference.** Every interval, scale, mode and chord page is generated and tested on all 35 roots, so correctness comes from tested code, not prose. |

Audio for every example is generated in the app: the Iowa MIS Steinway piano (public domain) plus synthesis.

Not known yet:
- open-licence samples for other timbres (Beato's ear course starts with timbre comparison);
- a safe source of public-domain score excerpts, since IMSLP editions can carry editorial rights.
