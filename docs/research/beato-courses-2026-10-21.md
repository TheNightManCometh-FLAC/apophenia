# Rick Beato's courses — structure study (21 Oct 2026)

Read from the public product pages on 21 Oct 2026: rickbeato.com, beatobook.com/pages/table-of-contents, beatoeartraining.com, songwriters.co, guitarscales.co. This is a summary in Claude's words of **layout, coverage and order** only, for plan §8 (Theory) and §9 (Pitch Trainer). None of his text, examples or audio is copied, and none may be.

## How the courses fit together
- Seven paid courses: the Beato Book (theory text), the Ear Training Method, the Scale Matrix, Quick Lessons Pro, the Arpeggio Masterclass, Music Theory for Songwriters, and the Beginner Guitar Course.
- His stated order is to do **the Book and Ear Training at the same time**: theory knowledge and hearing grow together. The songwriting and guitar courses come after, by skill level.

## The Beato Book (≈510 pages, interactive, audio examples, chapter videos)
Five chapters, in this order:
1. **Theory and harmony.**
   - Intervals and enharmonics, then the circle of fifths, then chord formulas.
   - The major scale with its triads and sevenths, its modal sounds, then building diatonic triads.
   - Minor scales, each with its modal sounds: natural, melodic and harmonic minor, then harmonic major and double harmonic.
   - More seventh chords, relative major/minor, chords for songwriters, modal interchange/mixture, then basic, advanced and suspended triads.
   - Chord scales and chord families, then scale-degree names, progressions, pre-dominant → dominant → tonic, cadences and Roman-numeral analysis.
   - Neapolitan, sus4 resolutions, backcycling, chord-substitution rules, multi-functional harmony.
   - Triads over bass notes and polychords, then reharmonization (basic → advanced → common-tone).
   - Modal harmony and modal modulation, voice leading, "mirror" modal equivalents, composing exercises.
2. **Chord forms (voicings).** Every triad type including sus and modal triads, drop-2/3/2+3/2+4 for 18 seventh-chord types, triads over every bass note, spread triads, quartal voicings (3–6 parts, major/melodic/harmonic minor), skipped-string, open-string, polychords, chordal scales, diminished voicings, stretch voicings, chordal lines.
3. **Scales and arpeggios practice.** Modes of the three parent scales, Messiaen modes, blues/pentatonic, fingerings, diatonic intervals and arpeggios, spread-triad and bitonal arpeggios.
4. **Improvisation.** ii–V–I, turnarounds, the cycle of fifths, pentatonics, triadic and seventh-chord superimposition, hybrid arpeggios, modal lines, blues/diminished/whole-tone sounds, outside playing, twelve-tone applications, studies.
5. **Bass lines, comping, solos over standard forms, and practising.**

## The Ear Training Method (20 chapters, 180 lessons, browser app, dark mode)
- **Order:**
  - Pitch and timbre, then intervals (harmonic and melodic, by family: 2nds, 3rds, 4ths/5ths, 6ths, 7ths, tritone).
  - Triads with inversions, sus and modal triads, then seventh chords including the unusual ones.
  - Tonal progressions (including a permutation trainer), secondary dominants and secondary sevenths, extended and added-note chords.
  - Rhythm dictation (quarters → eighths → sixteenths).
  - Scales: major, melodic-minor and harmonic-minor modes, symmetric, pentatonic/blues/bebop. Then modal voicings.
  - Melodic dictation, modal harmony, bitonal triads, reharmonization, polyrhythm, odd meter, polychords, twelve-tone, inner-note hearing, clusters.
- **Lessons compare a few things at a time** (e.g. "Dorian and Aeolian") and then build to "All …".
- **Every lesson has three modes:**
  - **Listen:** hear an example, see the answer, no score.
  - **Practice:** unlimited random examples with instant feedback.
  - **Test:** timed and scored.
- **Layout seen in the product screenshots:**
  - A chapter page is a table of lessons. Each row has the best score/time and three buttons, Listen / Practice / Test.
  - A lesson screen has a full-width coloured banner naming the mode, the lesson title, a replay/play/progress bar, a grid of answer buttons, then Next.
  - Melodic dictation is answered on an **on-screen piano**. A dashboard tracks scores and averages per chapter.
- Audio covers every key and register, so you learn the sound rather than memorise one example.

## Music Theory for Songwriters (19 lessons)
Order:
1. Sharp/flat keys.
2. Triads in a major key, then sevenths, then inversions.
3. Minor keys (natural, harmonic, melodic).
4. Secondary dominants.
5. Modes in songwriting, then modes in practice.
6. Borrowed chords.
7. Modulation, then chromatic-mediant modulation.
8. Pop/rock song forms.
9. Principles of melody.
10. Arranging, then breaking down a melody, then analysing hit songs.

## The Scale Matrix (guitar)
Order:
1. Pentatonic shapes in five positions, finding their roots, connecting them, then sequences.
2. The blues scale, licks across positions, then the formulas.
3. Hexatonic.
4. Major/minor in five positions.
5. Modes of major (shapes, then theory), and beyond.

## What this means for Apophenia (proposal, for Christopher)
- **Pair learning with hearing:** every Theory lesson ends with a **Check yourself** on the sounds it taught. The Pitch Trainer drills use Beato's three modes (Listen / Practice / Test). That pattern is a common one; the content is ours.
- Order the Theory course from sound and pitch through intervals, keys, triads, sevenths, modes, minor-family modes, harmony/Roman numerals/cadences, secondary dominants, modal interchange, voicings, reharmonization, modal modulation, and rhythm/meter alongside.
- Content must be **original or properly licensed**: Open Music Theory (CC BY-SA 4.0) can be adapted with attribution, and the audio is generated from the app's own piano samples.
